Archer weapons are catalogued in `public/equipment-visuals.js`: 5 existing Rare bows plus 25 individually generated framed icons across Epic, Unique, Legendary, Mythic, and Immortal. Each key is stable on the saved item and drives the equipped appearance, inventory art, and summon result. Rarity frame colors: Rare blue, Epic purple, Unique yellow, Legendary green, Mythic white, Immortal red. Generated catalog icons are optimized WebP under `public/assets/equipment/bows/`.
